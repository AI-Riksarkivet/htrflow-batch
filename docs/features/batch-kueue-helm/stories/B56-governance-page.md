# B56 · Governance page

**Story.** As an operator, I want a page documenting the rules for the campaigns repo and the deployment repo — protected branches, who approves what, CODEOWNERS, tokens and their owners — and why write access to either is cluster-operator access, so that the procedure exists in writing before anyone needs it under pressure.

## Why it matters

Governance that lives only in a git host's settings screen is invisible to an auditor and to the next admin.

## What this delivers

- A new page under the relevant track covering the rules for the campaigns repo and the deployment repo — protected branches, who approves what, CODEOWNERS, tokens and their owners — and why write access to either is cluster-operator access, linked from the nav and from the stories that need it.
- Commands shown are the real ones, tested once by following the page.

## Done when

- [ ] Page exists and is linked from the nav; a colleague follows it end to end without asking a question.
